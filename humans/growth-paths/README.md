# Growth Paths

How GTM humans level up when AI handles the drudgery — what the "AI-native" version of each role looks like.

The thesis: when agents handle 60-70% of the low-judgment work, humans have capacity to go deeper on the high-judgment work that moves revenue.

---

## What "leveling up" looks like by role

| Role | Before AI leverage | After AI leverage |
|------|------------------|------------------|
| SDR | Spends 4h/day on list building and sequence writing | Spends that time on calls and personalization |
| AE | Spends 2h/day on CRM updates and prep | Spends that time with customers and on deals |
| RevOps | Spends 3h/day on reports and data hygiene | Spends that time on system design and analysis |
| Demand Gen | Spends 2h/day on campaign assembly | Spends that time on strategy and creative direction |
| Content | Spends 3h/day on first drafts and repurposing | Spends that time on original insights and editing |
| PMM | Spends 2h/day on competitive monitoring | Spends that time on positioning and narrative |
