# Humans

AI-assisted GTM isn't about replacing humans — it's about giving them leverage on the parts of the job that drain them so they can do more of the parts that energize them.

This section catalogs what GTM humans actually do, where they bring irreplaceable judgment, and where AI can take drudgery off their plates.

---

## Three views

- [roles/](roles/) — what each GTM role does, where AI helps, where humans are essential
- [skills/](skills/) — human capabilities that agents can't replicate (judgment, relationship, narrative)
- [growth-paths/](growth-paths/) — how humans level up when AI handles the drudgery
