# SDR

The SDR owns the top of the funnel — finding the right people at the right accounts, getting their attention, and booking qualified meetings for AEs.

## Where SDRs bring joy

- **Persistence.** Following up the right number of times, in the right way, without being annoying.
- **First impression.** The opening message that gets a response — because it's specific and human.
- **Pattern recognition.** Learning which signals predict a good conversation and doubling down.
- **Phone presence.** The cold call that turns a skeptic into a meeting.

## What agents can take off their plate

| Drudgery task | How AI helps |
|--------------|-------------|
| Account research | Agent assembles a brief from Salesforce + LinkedIn + Gong history; SDR reads in 90 seconds |
| Email sequencing | Agent writes the sequence; SDR writes the P.S. and personal hook |
| List building | Agent pulls accounts matching ICP signals; SDR sanity-checks the top 20 |
| CRM hygiene | Agent logs activities and updates fields post-call; SDR confirms |
| Meeting prep handoff | Agent assembles the AE brief from the SDR's notes; SDR reviews before handing off |

## Where humans are irreplaceable

- **The personal hook.** The line that shows you read their LinkedIn post from Tuesday. Agents fake it; SDRs feel it.
- **Phone tone.** Warmth, humor, and adaptability on a cold call — real-time, unscripted.
- **Objection handling.** Knowing when to push back and when to retreat, based on real-time signals.
- **Relationship starter.** Being the first person at the company that the prospect likes.
