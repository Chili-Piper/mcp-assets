# AE

The Account Executive owns the deal — from first qualified conversation to closed-won. The relationship, the negotiation, the navigation of the buying committee.

## Where AEs bring joy

- **Relationship building.** Creating the trust that makes someone want to buy — and stay bought.
- **Reading the room.** Knowing when to push, when to listen, when to bring in an executive.
- **Negotiation.** Finding the deal structure that works for both sides.
- **Champion coaching.** Helping the champion sell internally when you're not in the room.
- **Personal hooks.** Writing the line in an email that only a human who did their homework could write.

## What agents can take off their plate

| Drudgery task | How AI helps |
|--------------|-------------|
| Pre-call research | Agent assembles account intel from Salesforce, LinkedIn, Gong — AE reviews in 2 min |
| Email drafting | Agent writes 80% of the body; AE writes the personal hook and approves |
| CRM updates post-call | Agent transcribes and logs Gong call notes; AE edits and confirms |
| Mutual action plan drafting | Agent creates first draft from call transcript; AE personalizes |
| Competitive battlecard lookup | Agent surfaces the right card at the right moment; AE decides how to use it |

## Where humans are irreplaceable

- **Trust.** Buyers buy from people they trust. Agents don't have a track record.
- **Negotiation judgment.** Knowing when to hold and when to flex — with full context on the relationship.
- **The personal hook.** The line that shows you actually did your homework. Agents approximate it; humans nail it.
- **Escalation calls.** When the deal is stuck, putting your VP on a call. Relationships open doors.
