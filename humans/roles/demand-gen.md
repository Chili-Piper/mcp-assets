# Demand Gen

Demand Gen owns pipeline creation — getting the right accounts into the funnel at the right time and with the right message.

## Where Demand Gen brings joy

- **Audience intuition.** Knowing which segments will respond to which messages before the data confirms it.
- **Narrative judgment.** Deciding what angle will cut through — not just what's technically accurate.
- **Channel mix.** Understanding which channels are over-indexed and under-indexed for the target audience.
- **Brand voice.** Keeping the message on-brand even when scaling at volume.

## What agents can take off their plate

| Drudgery task | How AI helps |
|--------------|-------------|
| Account list building | Agent pulls from Salesforce + Clay based on ICP criteria; DG reviews |
| Campaign performance reporting | Agent assembles weekly performance data; DG interprets and acts |
| A/B test setup | Agent writes variant copy; DG selects, approves, and reads results |
| Content adaptation (repurposing) | Agent adapts long-form to multiple formats; DG edits for voice |
| Audience segmentation | Agent scores and clusters; DG validates and names segments |

## Where humans are irreplaceable

- **Narrative creation.** The insight that makes someone stop scrolling — that's human.
- **Trend judgment.** Spotting which cultural moment is safe to engage with and which isn't.
- **Brand intuition.** Knowing when something is technically correct but feels off-brand.
- **Relationship with creative.** Briefing designers and writers in a way that produces great work.
