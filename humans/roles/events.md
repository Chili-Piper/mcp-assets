# Events

Events owns the human moments — the experiences that build relationships, generate pipeline, and accelerate deals in ways that digital channels can't replicate.

## Where Events brings joy

- **Experience design.** Knowing what kind of experience will make someone feel something.
- **Relationship building.** The dinner, the conversation, the follow-up that turns an attendee into a champion.
- **Logistics judgment.** The thousand micro-decisions that turn a venue into an experience.
- **Energy management.** Reading a room and adjusting the agenda in real time.
- **Vendor relationships.** The network that gets you the right venue at the right price.

## What agents can take off their plate

| Drudgery task | How AI helps |
|--------------|-------------|
| Attendee list building | Agent pulls target accounts + contacts from Salesforce matching ICP; events team reviews |
| Invite personalization | Agent drafts personalized invites; human writes the P.S. and sends from their account |
| Post-event follow-up | Agent drafts follow-up sequences; event marketer reviews and personalizes top accounts |
| ROI reporting | Agent assembles pipeline influenced by event from Salesforce; events team tells the story |
| Venue research | Agent compiles options matching criteria; events team evaluates and visits |

## Where humans are irreplaceable

- **The moment.** The conversation that happens over dinner that you could never script.
- **Energy.** A room that feels alive versus one that feels corporate — that's a human skill.
- **Relationship judgment.** Knowing which attendees need a host's attention and which are fine on their own.
- **Vendor negotiation.** The give-and-take that gets you a better rate and a better room.
