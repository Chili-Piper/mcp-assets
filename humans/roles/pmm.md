# PMM

Product Marketing owns the story — why the product exists, who it's for, why now, and why us over the alternative. The PMM's job is to make the market care.

## Where PMMs bring joy

- **Positioning judgment.** Finding the frame that makes your product obviously the right choice.
- **Competitive intuition.** Knowing which competitor moves to respond to and which to ignore.
- **Narrative architecture.** Building the story arc that takes a buyer from unaware to convinced.
- **Launch orchestration.** Aligning sales, CS, product, and marketing behind a single story.
- **Win/loss insight.** Synthesizing why deals are won and lost into something actionable.

## What agents can take off their plate

| Drudgery task | How AI helps |
|--------------|-------------|
| Competitive monitoring | Agent tracks competitor announcements, reviews, and job postings; PMM interprets |
| Customer interview synthesis | Agent transcribes and clusters themes; PMM finds the insight |
| Sales enablement content | Agent drafts battlecards and one-pagers from positioning doc; PMM edits |
| Win/loss report assembly | Agent pulls Gong + Salesforce data into a structured report; PMM tells the story |
| Persona research | Agent aggregates signals; PMM validates against direct customer conversations |

## Where humans are irreplaceable

- **Positioning judgment.** Which frame resonates — and more importantly, which frame is defensible.
- **The customer insight.** The thing you only learn from sitting with customers, not reading transcripts.
- **Launch sequencing.** Knowing which assets to release when, given internal readiness and market timing.
- **Cross-functional storytelling.** Getting product and sales to agree on a single narrative.
