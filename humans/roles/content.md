# Content

The Content role owns the editorial program — the ideas, the voice, the quality bar, and the distribution strategy that makes content worth reading.

## Where Content brings joy

- **Voice.** The specific way your brand sounds — which no AI fully replicates without a strong human editor.
- **Editorial judgment.** Knowing which idea is worth 2,000 words and which needs to be killed.
- **Audience empathy.** Writing from the reader's perspective, not the company's.
- **Originality.** The insight or angle that hasn't been said yet.
- **Quality bar.** Knowing the difference between "good enough for a draft" and "good enough to publish."

## What agents can take off their plate

| Drudgery task | How AI helps |
|--------------|-------------|
| First-draft writing | Agent produces a structured draft; human editor shapes it into something worth reading |
| Content repurposing | Agent adapts long-form to LinkedIn, newsletter, Twitter thread; editor edits for each channel |
| SEO brief writing | Agent researches keywords and outlines the structure; human writes the content |
| Content calendar population | Agent suggests topics based on themes and gaps; editor curates |
| Distribution copy | Agent writes the promotion copy; editor tightens and adds voice |

## Where humans are irreplaceable

- **The original insight.** The thing that makes someone forward the piece to a colleague.
- **Voice.** Real voice — not approximated voice — builds the audience that converts.
- **Editorial taste.** Knowing what's good. Agents optimize for readability scores; editors optimize for impact.
- **Interview and sourcing.** The quotes, the data, the examples that make a piece credible.
