# Orchestration Recipes

The Chili Piper-shaped zone. Recipes for coordinating humans + agents at the moments that matter most — handoffs between teams, routing to the right person, escalating when something needs immediate human attention.

**What makes these different:** Every orchestration recipe makes the human-agent loop explicit. Who hands off to whom, with what context, in what time window.

---

## Sub-categories

- [Handoff](handoff/) — pass the baton between humans and agents with full context
- [Routing](routing/) — get the right lead/account/request to the right human fast
- [Escalation](escalation/) — surface what needs a human, right now
