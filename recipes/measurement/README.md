# Measurement Recipes

Recipes for campaign attribution and optimization — closing the loop between GTM activity and revenue outcomes.

**What these move:** Improves attribution accuracy, campaign ROI, and budget allocation decisions

**Metrics to watch:** Attribution coverage, revenue-per-campaign, optimization cycle time

---

## Why measurement is a first-class category

Every recipe in this repo requires a `measurement` block. But some recipes are *about* measurement — building the infrastructure that makes other recipes optimizable. Those live here.
