# Education Recipes

Recipes for teaching aware accounts that they have a problem worth solving — and that you understand it better than anyone.

**What these move:** Aware → engaged / educated

**Metrics to watch:** Content engagement rate, time-on-page, return visits, email open/click rates

## What makes a good education recipe

- Personalizes content to account-level signals (industry, size, tech stack)
- Agent surfaces the right asset at the right time; human crafts the narrative frame
- Measurement: content engagement tracked back to account in CRM
