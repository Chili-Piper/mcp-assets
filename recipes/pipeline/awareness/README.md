## What makes a good awareness recipe

- Identifies the right accounts to target (ICP + timing signals)
- Uses intent data or social signals, not just static lists
- Agent handles the signal processing; human approves the outreach
- Measurement: what campaign member record gets created in Salesforce/HubSpot
