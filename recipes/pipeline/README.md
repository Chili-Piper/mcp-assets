# Pipeline Recipes

Recipes that move accounts through the bowtie funnel — from cold to closed-won.

The bowtie model has two sides:
- **Left side (new pipeline):** Awareness → Education → Selection
- **Right side (NRR):** Onboarding → Impact → Expansion ← *our differentiated zone*

Most "AI for GTM" tools play on the left side (drafting emails, scoring leads). Our defensible wedge is the **right side** — orchestrating humans + agents to protect and grow NRR. No leaky bucket.

## Stages

- [Awareness](awareness/) — get in front of the right accounts at the right time
- [Education](education/) — teach them why they have a problem worth solving
- [Selection](selection/) — help them choose you over alternatives
- [Onboarding](onboarding/) — get them to first value fast
- [Impact](impact/) — prove the ROI so it's a no-brainer to stay
- [Expansion](expansion/) — grow NRR, expand to more seats/products/teams
