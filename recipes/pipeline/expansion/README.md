# Expansion Recipes

The differentiated zone. Recipes for growing NRR — expanding to more seats, more products, more teams — and plugging the leaky bucket before churn happens.

**What these move:** Retained customer → expanded / upsold

**Metrics to watch:** Expansion rate, NRR, logo churn rate, expansion pipeline coverage

---

## Why this stage is our wedge

Most "AI for GTM" content is generation-side — drafting emails, scoring net-new leads. Expansion is under-indexed. Orchestrating humans + agents to grow existing accounts and prevent churn is where Chili Piper specifically adds value (routing, scheduling, handoff — all the moments that matter for NRR).

## What makes a good expansion recipe

- Identifies expansion signals early (usage spikes, new team members, champion job changes)
- Separates "likely to expand" from "at-risk of churn" with different response plays
- Agent monitors signals and drafts outreach; CS or AE owns the conversation
- Measurement: expansion opportunity created in Salesforce with source attribution
