## What makes a good selection recipe

- Multi-threads across the buying committee (not just the champion)
- Surfaces competitive intel at the right moment
- Agent handles research and draft; AE handles the relationship and final framing
- Measurement: win/loss outcome written to opportunity in Salesforce
