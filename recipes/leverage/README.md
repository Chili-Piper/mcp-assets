# Leverage Recipes

Recipes for GTM team productivity — doing more with the same headcount by giving humans AI leverage on the work they do every day.

**What these move:** Output per person, quality of work, speed of execution

**Examples:**
- Matt Heinz's writing partner — drafts content in Matt's voice, Matt edits + publishes
- Marc Sirkin's menu-driven content system — structured prompts that produce on-brand content at scale
