# Contributors

The people who built the recipes, skills, and agents in this cookbook.

---

## How to add your profile

Create a file at `contributors/<your-github-handle>.md`:

```markdown
---
name: Your Name
handle: your-github-handle
role: Your GTM role (e.g. RevOps, Demand Gen, AE)
company: Your company (optional)
linkedin: https://linkedin.com/in/yourprofile (optional)
---

One paragraph about you and your GTM perspective. What do you care about? What are you trying to solve?

upsell: https://your-link.com (optional — link to your templates, courses, newsletter, or services)
```

### On the `upsell` field

You can link to your paid offerings. We don't take a cut. We want MKT1-tier creators here — people who have built real audiences and real products. If your recipe is good, the upsell link is earned.
